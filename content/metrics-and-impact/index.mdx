## Why Metrics Matter in Interviews

Strong metrics demonstrate:

| Without Metrics | With Metrics |
|----------------|--------------|
| "Improved performance" | "Reduced latency by 40% (p99: 500ms → 300ms)" |
| "Scaled the system" | "Scaled to handle 10x traffic (100K → 1M RPM)" |
| "Saved engineering time" | "Reduced deployment time from 2 hours to 15 minutes" |
| "Reduced bugs" | "Decreased production incidents by 60% (20 → 8/month)" |

## Impact Quantification Framework

1. **Baseline** – What was the situation before?
2. **Change** – What did you do?
3. **Outcome** – What improved?
4. **Attribution** – Why was it your contribution?
5. **Business Value** – What does it mean for the business?
