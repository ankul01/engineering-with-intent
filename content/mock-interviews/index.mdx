## Mock Interview Best Practices

### How to Practice Effectively

1. **Simulate real conditions** – Timed, uninterrupted, professional setting
2. **Record yourself** – Watch for filler words, pacing, clarity
3. **Get external feedback** – Peers, mentors, professional coaches
4. **Track patterns** – What mistakes keep recurring?
5. **Iterate** – Focus on weaknesses, not just strengths

### Feedback Capture Template

After each mock interview, capture:

- **Date & Type** – When and what kind of interview
- **Questions Asked** – What were you asked?
- **What Went Well** – Strengths to maintain
- **What to Improve** – Specific areas to work on
- **Action Items** – Concrete next steps

### Recommended Practice Schedule

| Weeks Out | Focus Area |
|-----------|------------|
| 8+ weeks | Foundation building, fill knowledge gaps |
| 4-8 weeks | Intensive practice, multiple mocks per week |
| 2-4 weeks | Polish and refine, focus on weak areas |
| 1 week | Light review, confidence building |
| Day before | Rest and mental preparation |
