## Key Interview Questions

- "Tell me about a time you failed"
- "Describe your biggest mistake"
- "What would you do differently?"

## Tips for Failure Stories

- **Own the failure** – Don't deflect blame
- **Show learning** – What did you take away?
- **Demonstrate growth** – How did you change?
