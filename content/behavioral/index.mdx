## The STAR Method

Structure your answers using STAR for clarity and impact:

| Component | What to Include | Tips |
|-----------|----------------|------|
| **S**ituation | Context and background | Keep it brief, 1-2 sentences |
| **T**ask | Your specific responsibility | Clarify YOUR role |
| **A**ction | What YOU did (not the team) | Use "I", be specific |
| **R**esult | Quantifiable outcomes | Use numbers when possible |

### Story Bank Essentials

Prepare 6-8 polished stories that can flex across multiple competencies:

- **2-3 Leadership stories** – Vision, influence, team development
- **1-2 Failure stories** – With clear learnings and recovery
- **1-2 Conflict stories** – Cross-team, manager, or stakeholder
- **1-2 Impact stories** – Measurable business outcomes

### Common Mistakes

1. **Too much "we"** – Interviewers want to know what YOU did
2. **No metrics** – Quantify impact whenever possible
3. **Missing the "so what"** – Connect to business value
4. **Too long** – Aim for 2-3 minutes per story
5. **Not answering the question** – Stay relevant to what's asked

### Preparation Tips

- **Practice out loud** – Timing and flow matter
- **Record yourself** – Listen for filler words and clarity
- **Get feedback** – Do mock interviews with peers
- **Vary your stories** – Don't repeat the same example
- **Update regularly** – Add recent wins and learnings
