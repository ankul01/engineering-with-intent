# Hiring

Hiring is the highest-leverage activity for engineering leaders. A great hire multiplies team effectiveness; a bad hire drains it. This guide covers building effective interview processes and making high-quality hiring decisions.

## Interview Process Design

### End-to-End Flow

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   Hiring Funnel                              │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                              │
│  Sourcing ──▶ Screen ──▶ Phone ──▶ Onsite ──▶ Offer ──▶ Close│
│                                                              │
│  100        30         15        5         2         1      │
│  candidates reviewed  screens   onsites   offers    hire    │
│                                                              │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Interview Loop Structure

| Round | Duration | Focus | Interviewers |
|-------|----------|-------|--------------|
| **Recruiter Screen** | 30 min | Logistics, motivation, salary | Recruiter |
| **Technical Screen** | 45-60 min | Coding fundamentals | Engineer |
| **System Design** | 60 min | Architecture skills | Senior engineer |
| **Coding Deep Dive** | 60 min | Problem-solving, code quality | Engineers (2) |
| **Behavioral** | 45-60 min | Leadership, collaboration | Manager + cross-functional |
| **Hiring Manager** | 45 min | Team fit, expectations, Q&A | Hiring manager |

## Interview Rubrics

### Rating Scale

| Score | Meaning | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| **1 - Strong No** | Significant concerns | Do not hire |
| **2 - Lean No** | Below bar for level | Do not hire |
| **3 - Lean Yes** | Meets bar, some gaps | Discuss in debrief |
| **4 - Strong Yes** | Exceeds expectations | Hire |

### Coding Interview Rubric

| Dimension | Strong No (1) | Lean No (2) | Lean Yes (3) | Strong Yes (4) |
|-----------|---------------|-------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Problem Solving** | Couldn't make progress | Needed significant hints | Solved with minor hints | Solved independently |
| **Code Quality** | Unreadable, many bugs | Some issues, incomplete | Clean, minor issues | Clean, well-structured |
| **Communication** | Couldn't explain | Unclear explanations | Clear with prompting | Excellent, proactive |
| **Testing** | No consideration | Basic cases only | Good coverage | Comprehensive edge cases |

### System Design Rubric

| Dimension | Strong No (1) | Lean No (2) | Lean Yes (3) | Strong Yes (4) |
|-----------|---------------|-------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Requirements** | Didn't clarify | Missed key points | Covered basics | Thorough, insightful |
| **Architecture** | Fundamentally flawed | Major gaps | Sound, minor gaps | Excellent, scalable |
| **Tradeoffs** | No awareness | Superficial | Good discussion | Deep analysis |
| **Depth** | Surface only | Some depth | Good depth | Expert-level |

### Behavioral Rubric (STAR)

| Dimension | Strong No (1) | Lean No (2) | Lean Yes (3) | Strong Yes (4) |
|-----------|---------------|-------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Situation** | Vague, unclear | Basic context | Clear context | Rich detail |
| **Task** | Role unclear | Some clarity | Clear ownership | Full accountability |
| **Action** | Minimal action | Limited impact | Strong actions | Exceptional initiative |
| **Result** | No outcome | Unclear impact | Positive outcome | Measurable impact |

## Conducting Effective Interviews

### Interview Structure

```
0-5 min:   Introduction, put candidate at ease
5-10 min:  Set expectations for the interview
10-50 min: Core interview content
50-55 min: Candidate questions
55-60 min: Wrap up, explain next steps
```

### Best Practices

| Do | Don't |
|-----|-------|
| Put candidate at ease | Jump straight into questions |
| Explain format upfront | Keep them guessing |
| Give hints when stuck | Let them struggle silently |
| Take notes in real-time | Rely on memory |
| Evaluate against rubric | Compare to other candidates |
| Focus on signal, not tricks | Use gotcha questions |

### Bias Mitigation

| Bias | Description | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|------------|
| **Halo effect** | One good trait colors all | Separate rubric dimensions |
| **Similar-to-me** | Favor similar backgrounds | Structured questions |
| **Recency** | Remember recent answers | Take notes throughout |
| **Confirmation** | Seek confirming evidence | Look for disconfirming data |
| **Anchoring** | First impression dominates | Score each dimension independently |

## Debrief and Decision Making

### Debrief Structure

```
1. Independent Write-up (before meeting)
   ├── Each interviewer writes feedback
   ├── Assigns scores per dimension
   └── Overall recommendation

2. Debrief Meeting
   ├── Round-robin: Each interviewer shares
   ├── Focus on evidence, not conclusions
   ├── Discuss discrepancies
   └── Hiring manager makes final call

3. Documentation
   ├── Record decision and rationale
   ├── Store for future reference
   └── Feedback to recruiters
```

### Decision Framework

```
Strong Yes + Strong Yes = Likely Hire
Strong Yes + Lean Yes = Likely Hire
Lean Yes + Lean Yes = Discuss carefully
Any Strong No = Usually No Hire
Mixed signals = Dig deeper, may need additional interview
```

### Calibration

| Activity | Frequency | Purpose |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| **Shadow interviews** | Ongoing | Train new interviewers |
| **Calibration sessions** | Monthly | Align on bar |
| **Interview review** | Quarterly | Improve questions |
| **Hiring retrospective** | Quarterly | Review quality of hires |

## Closing Candidates

### Sell Points (know your strengths)

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Mission** | Impact, meaningful work |
| **Technology** | Interesting problems, modern stack |
| **Team** | Quality of people, culture |
| **Growth** | Learning, career progression |
| **Compensation** | Total package, equity upside |
| **Work-Life** | Flexibility, balance |

### Closing Conversation

```
1. Understand priorities
   "What's most important to you in your next role?"

2. Address concerns
   "What questions or concerns do you have?"

3. Share relevant stories
   "Let me tell you about someone who joined in a similar situation..."

4. Create urgency (honestly)
   "We'd love to have your decision by [date] because..."

5. Make the ask
   "Based on our conversations, I think you'd be a great fit. 
    Are you ready to join us?"
```

## Interview Quick Reference

### Common Interview Questions (for you as a leader)

1. **"How do you build an effective interview process?"**
   - Define competencies and rubrics
   - Train interviewers on bias
   - Structure interviews consistently
   - Calibrate regularly

2. **"How do you raise the hiring bar?"**
   - Clear level expectations
   - Calibration sessions
   - Data on hire quality
   - Hold interviewers accountable

3. **"Tell me about a difficult hiring decision"**
   - Use STAR format
   - Show data-driven approach
   - Discuss tradeoffs explicitly
   - Share outcome and learnings

### Hiring Metrics

| Metric | Target | Why |
|--------|--------|-----|
| Time to hire | 30-45 days | Speed vs thoroughness |
| Offer acceptance rate | 80%+ | Closing effectiveness |
| Interview-to-offer | 4-5:1 | Process efficiency |
| Quality of hire | 90%+ meet bar at 6mo | Ultimate measure |
| Diversity | Improve YoY | Inclusive hiring |

### Checklist

- [ ] Rubrics defined for each round?
- [ ] Interviewers trained and calibrated?
- [ ] Bias mitigation in place?
- [ ] Debrief process structured?
- [ ] Candidate experience positive?
- [ ] Closing strategy prepared?
