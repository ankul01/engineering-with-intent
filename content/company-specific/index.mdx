|---------|---------------|-------------|
| Google | 5-6 | Coding, System Design, Googleyness |

| Microsoft | 4-5 | Behavioral, Technical, As Appropriate |

## Preparation Tips

- **Research culture** – Understand company values and how they're evaluated
- **Tailor stories** – Map your experiences to company-specific principles
- **Practice format** – Each company has unique interview styles
- **Read recent experiences** – Interview processes change frequently
