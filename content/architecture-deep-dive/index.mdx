## Why Deep Dives Matter

At senior and staff levels, interviewers expect you to:

- **Explain the "why"** – Not just what to use, but why it works
- **Understand tradeoffs** – Every decision has consequences
- **Connect concepts** – See how systems interact end-to-end
- **Debug from first principles** – Diagnose issues without Google
- **Make informed decisions** – Choose technologies based on understanding

### Interview Signals

| Level | Expected Depth |
|-------|---------------|
| **Senior** | Solid understanding of core concepts, can explain tradeoffs |
| **Staff** | Deep expertise, can design novel solutions, teaches others |
| **Principal** | Industry-level knowledge, influences technical direction |
