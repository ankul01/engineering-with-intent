## Why Networking Matters

Understanding networking fundamentals is essential for:

- **System Design** – Make informed decisions about architecture
- **Debugging** – Diagnose latency, connectivity, and performance issues
- **Security** – Understand attack vectors and defense mechanisms
- **Performance** – Optimize for global scale and user experience

### Interview Context

At senior levels, expect questions like:
- "Walk me through what happens when you type a URL in the browser"
- "How would you debug a 500ms latency spike for users in Asia?"
- "Design a system that serves content globally with \<100ms latency"
