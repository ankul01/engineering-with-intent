# Leadership Learning

Interview preparation repository for senior engineering and engineering leadership roles.

## Focus Areas

- **Platform Thinking** – Building scalable, maintainable systems
- **Scalability** – Designing for growth and high availability
- **Risk & Compliance** – Security, data governance, regulatory requirements
- **Stakeholder Alignment** – Communication, influence, and collaboration
- **Execution Excellence** – Delivery, prioritization, and trade-offs
- **Business Impact** – Metrics-driven decision making

## Content Categories

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| [Behavioral](/behavioral) | Structured stories and frameworks for behavioral rounds |
| [System Design](/system-design) | High-level and low-level design problems, patterns |
| [Coding](/coding) | DSA patterns, problem-solving frameworks |
| [Frameworks](/frameworks) | STAR method, decision trees, tradeoff matrices |
| [Architecture](/architecture-deep-dive) | Networking, reliability, security deep dives |

## Preparation Strategy

- **Behavioral depth over breadth** – Deep stories with measurable impact
- **System design with tradeoffs** – Not just solutions, but reasoning
- **Engineering leadership narratives** – Hiring, performance, conflict resolution
- **Metrics-first storytelling** – Numbers that demonstrate business impact
