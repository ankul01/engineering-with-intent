# Stakeholder Alignment

Getting buy-in from product, business, and engineering stakeholders. Managing competing priorities.

## Topics

### Identifying Stakeholders
Who needs to be involved, who needs to be informed, and who has veto power.

### Building Buy-In
Pre-meetings, one-on-ones, and coalition building.

### Managing Competing Priorities
When stakeholders disagree, prioritization frameworks, and escalation.

### Communication Strategies
Technical translation, executive summaries, and visual communication.

### Ongoing Alignment
Status updates, progress sharing, and relationship maintenance.
