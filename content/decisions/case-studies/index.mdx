# Case Studies

Sanitized real-world decisions with full context, constraints, and outcomes.

## Format

Each case study includes:

1. **Context** — Business and technical situation
2. **Constraints** — What shaped the decision space
3. **Decision** — What was chosen
4. **Outcome** — What happened
5. **Lessons** — What we'd do differently
