## Day-Before Checklist

- [ ] Review your top 6-8 behavioral stories
- [ ] Practice explaining your most complex project
- [ ] Review key system design patterns
- [ ] Get good sleep (seriously!)
- [ ] Prepare questions for interviewers
- [ ] Test your setup (for virtual interviews)

## Interview Day Tips

- **Arrive early** – Whether virtual or in-person
- **Think out loud** – Communication is key
- **Ask clarifying questions** – Don't assume
- **Stay calm** – It's okay to take a moment to think
- **Be yourself** – Authenticity matters
