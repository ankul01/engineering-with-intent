## What is LLD?

Low-Level Design focuses on:

- **Class design** – Entities, relationships, and responsibilities
- **Design patterns** – Applying proven solutions to common problems
- **Interface design** – APIs, contracts, and extensibility
- **Data structures** – Choosing appropriate internal representations

## Interview Approach

1. **Clarify requirements** – What features to support?
2. **Identify entities** – What are the main objects?
3. **Define relationships** – How do objects interact?
4. **Apply patterns** – Strategy, Factory, Observer, etc.
5. **Write code** – Clean, testable implementation
